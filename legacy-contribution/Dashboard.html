Logic & Filters

Target Population

Reports are filtered for Services Team Contributors and Crew Members. Specifically targets Job Families of Managed Services & Support and Service Delivery.

PTO vs Admin Classification

Non-billable time logged as - No Customer/Project is categorized as PTO only if:

  • A record exists in Time Off for that date.
  • OR it matches a PH entry for the Rocketeer's location.

All other "No Customer" time is flagged as Admin Burden.

WoW Calculation

W0 (Current): Last full ISO week.
W1 (Previous): Week prior to W0.
W2 (Baseline): Week prior to W1 (used for 3-week trend lines).

KPI Definitions

Contribution Health Index (CHI)

A composite 0-100% score based on 3 signals:

  • S1 (Captured Value): % of Done Jira items that have billable hours.
  • S2 (Tracked Efficiency): The gap between OpenAir/Tempo hours and NetSuite Billed hours.
  • S3 (Focus): % of logged effort aligned with documented backlog priorities.

Attainment %

Actual Billable Hours / Weekly Target (default 32h). Status is On Track if attainment ≥ 90%.
Total Availability: Baseline capacity calculation for operations.

Security & Authorization

Access restricted via Google Email whitelist. Every data request and Q&A interaction is verified server-side. Unauthorized attempts are logged and blocked.
