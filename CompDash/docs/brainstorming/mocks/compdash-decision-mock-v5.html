How the person-level signal is decided

Four states, one hierarchy: the market that can actually poach the person governs the signal. Internal percentile is context only — small groups and mixed titles misclassify people, so an internal-only gap can never raise more than NORMAL. And for remote-servable roles, "market" means the remote-pull benchmark (what US/global remote employers pay for that role in that country), not the local in-country market — local can flatter while the aggressive market is already recruiting.
SignalRuleWhat it triggers
◆ COMPETITIVE≥ 1.0× remote-pull benchmarkNothing — pay is a moat; protect via growth, not money
✓ STABLE0.85–1.0× remote-pull, no compounding factorStandard QTA cadence
! NORMALBelow internal group median but ≥ 0.85× remote-pullNote in cycle · verify the anchor (possible group misclassification) — internal-only, never escalates alone
✕ RISK< 0.85× remote-pull benchmarkQTA review queued this cycle
🔥 RISK · IMMEDIATERISK and high performer (SI streak ≥ 4, or on acceleration track)Manager + WFP follow-up this week — high performers get poached first; the queue does not apply to them
Remote-pull benchmarks: Benchmarking Engine v9 remote-remuneration set (US-employer LATAM/APAC remote offers), refreshed quarterly. Where no remote set exists for a role, the signal falls back to local market and says so.
