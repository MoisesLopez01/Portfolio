Data Reference

Data sources, metric logic, and evaluation rules used in this dashboard

Purpose

The PS Weekly Contribution Report consolidates key engagement and performance metrics for the Professional Services team across a rolling 5-week window. It is designed to give PS leadership a clear, weekly view of team health — what is being delivered, how goals are progressing, and where individuals may need support.

Data is refreshed weekly. The dashboard reflects exports as of the last update date shown at the top of the page — it is not a live feed. Most metrics cover the past two completed weeks plus the current week; some, like RCR and SI, are updated on a monthly or quarterly cycle.

Metrics Explained
RCR — Revenue Contribution Rate
Revenue generated by the team divided by total cash compensation for the most recently completed month. This is a team-level indicator — it tells us how much revenue we produce relative to our cost as a group, not a measure of any individual. We aim to stay above 1×; the higher, the better. The model may evolve in upcoming quarters as we refine how revenue is attributed, so treat this as a directional check rather than a hard target.
Updated monthly · reflects the last closed month shown at the top of the card.
SI — Small Improvements Rating
Quarterly manager rating on a 1–5 scale from the Small Improvements platform. The dashboard shows the FY26Q3 result as a rounded integer. This is a static value — it does not change week to week until a new quarter closes.
Scale: 0 Not rated (excluded) · 1 Needs Help · 2 Below Expectation · 3 Meeting · 4 Great · 5 Outstanding
Goals connected
Weekly compliance check against FY26Q4 goals in the RD sheet. A rocketeer is compliant if they pass all three rules (see the Goals Evaluation section below). A connected rocketeer has tied their work to something that matters — goals drive planning, planning drives execution. The dashboard evaluates each past and current week independently.
Available for weeks W-2 → W0 (current). Future weeks show —.
Time planned
Hours that PS team members have planned in Tempo for each week. A team that plans its work sets itself up to deliver — planning is a decision, not a default. The dashboard shows planned hours across all 5 weeks, including historical weeks and upcoming ones.
Available across all 5 weeks (past, current, and W+1, W+2).
Time tracked
Hours logged by PS team members in Tempo each week. Hours are summed per complete ISO calendar week (Mon–Sun) and matched to each rocketeer on the roster. Includes all PS team members — ramp-up and active alike.
Available for weeks W-2 → W0 (current).
Kudos given
Count of Kudos received by PS team members each week. Recognizing someone's contribution is a team behavior we want to grow — this metric surfaces how much the team sees and appreciates each other.
Available for weeks W-2 → W0 (current).
PTO — Planned Time Off
Each employee is evaluated as Planned (PTO balance under control, or upcoming leave already booked) or Needs a plan (high accumulated balance with no upcoming leave). Planning rest is a professional skill — a team that plans its recovery sustains its performance. Team score = % of employees in Planned status. Available across all 5 weeks, including forward weeks, giving a proactive view of team wellbeing.
Available all 5 weeks including W+1 and W+2.
Goals Evaluation — FY26Q4

Each week, every rocketeer is evaluated against their FY26Q4 goals. To be marked Compliant, all three rules below must pass. The dashboard evaluates each week independently using that week's Mon–Sun window. Quarter target: 10 goals, 8 completed by June 30 2026.

1
Goal Count — at least 8 goals
The rocketeer must have at least 8 distinct goals in their FY26Q4 goal set. The quarter-end target is 10 goals with 8 completed — 8 is the minimum to be evaluated week over week.
2
In Progress — majority of goals actively in progress
More than 50% of goals must have a status of In Progress. Planned or Completed goals do not count toward this rule. This ensures active execution, not just goal setup.
3
Comment Engagement — at least 6 goals with cumulative self-comments
At least 6 goals must have the required number of cumulative self-comments written by the rocketeer by that week's end. Comments from managers do not count. The required comment count per goal ramps up as Q4 progresses.
How the required comment count grows through Q4:
Formula: max(1, quarterWeek − 4) where Q4 week 1 = 2026.W14.

2026.W14 – W18 (Q4 weeks 1–5): 1 comment per goal
2026.W19 (week 6): 2 comments per goal
2026.W20 (week 7): 3 comments per goal
2026.W21 (week 8): 4 comments per goal
2026.W22 (week 9): 5 comments per goal
2026.W23+ (week 10+): 6 comments per goal

This gradual ramp encourages consistent engagement throughout the quarter rather than a last-minute push.
When the detail row is expanded for an employee, you can see exactly which rule failed — for example, "Rule 2: 3/8 goals In Progress (need 5)" or "Rule 3: 4/6 goals with required comments (need 6, each needing 2 comments this quarter)".

Team Radar — Axis Definitions

The Team Radar (snowflake chart) plots seven axes on a 0–100 scale for any selected group and reference week. Each axis normalises its raw value differently. The "Evaluate Week" selector controls which week is used for single-week axes (Goals, Tracked compliance, Planned compliance, Kudos, PTO Health); period averages (Tracked avg, Planned avg) use the full 5-week window.

Week Range

All weekly data uses ISO 8601 week numbering — weeks run Monday to Sunday. Week labels follow the format YYYY.WN, for example 2026.W20 covers Mon 11 May – Sun 17 May 2026. The 5-week window is always centered on the current ISO week: two past weeks, the current week, and two future weeks.
