When a menu page is open, what does the header show?

You navigated via ☰ to Access Control. The tab bar has no active tab — how do you see where you are, and how do you get back? Both wireframes show the closed-menu state.

A

Page chip next to the hamburger

The ☰ button stays highlighted and the current page name appears as a chip beside it, with an × to close and jump back to Overview. Tab bar stays untouched.

B

Temporary closable tab in the tab bar

Opening a menu page appends it to the tab bar as an active tab with an ×, like a browser/IDE tab. Closing it (or clicking another tab) removes it. Familiar pattern, but the tab bar width shifts as pages open and close.
