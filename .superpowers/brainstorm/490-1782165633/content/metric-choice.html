What should the project-context trend measure?

Planned hours week-over-week is already covered (Time Momentum + Chart B). So this card should trend project variety / context, not hours. With 80+ projects a week, individual keys can't be the unit — we need one team-level number per week that a CEO reads in two seconds. Three candidates, drawn over the 7-week window (W18→W24, W21 = current):

A — Avg projects per person (context-switching load)

Total distinct projects per person, averaged across the team. Rising = the team is spreading across more contexts each; falling = consolidating focus.

CEO reads: "Are my people getting stretched thinner?"

B — Distinct active projects (portfolio breadth)

Count of unique projects the whole team is planned on each week. Shows how many fronts the organization is engaged on — breadth of the delivery portfolio.

CEO reads: "How many engagements are we running concurrently?"

C — Both, on one chart (breadth + load)

Both lines together (dual scale). Richer, but two metrics at once is more than a single C-suite glance usually wants.

CEO reads: two things — needs a moment longer.
