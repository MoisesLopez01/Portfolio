Data Reference

Data sources, metric logic, and evaluation rules used in this dashboard

Purpose

The PS Weekly Contribution Report consolidates key engagement and performance metrics for the Professional Services team across a rolling 5-week window. It is designed to give PS leadership a clear, weekly view of team health — what is being delivered, how goals are progressing, and where individuals may need support.

Data is refreshed weekly. The dashboard reflects exports as of the last update date shown at the top of the page — it is not a live feed. Most metrics cover the past two completed weeks plus the current week; some, like RCR and SI, are updated on a monthly or quarterly cycle.

Page visits are logged (who and when) to measure adoption. Usage analytics are visible to super admins only.

Ramp-Up employees (hired within the last 3 months, or flagged as Ramp-Up in the roster) are shown in the employee table with an amber badge, but are excluded from performance metrics — RCR, SI, Goals Commented, and Kudos given. They remain included in time-based metrics: Tracked Time, Planned Time, and PTO.
🏖 On Leave — anyone whose Employee Status names maternity, paternity, parental, sabbatical, or a leave of absence stays in the employee table with a badge, but leaves every team metric until they return. An absence is not a performance signal.
⏱ Time-tracking mandate — only JF007 Managed Services & Support and JF009 Service Delivery are held to a planning and tracking standard, along with crew and anyone whose job title does not resolve to a job family (an unrecognised title never grants an exemption). Everyone else in the Services roster — JF008 Training & Customer Education, JF015 Operations, JF017 Information Technology, JF018 Solution Architecture and the embedded operational families — carries an ⏱ Exempt badge. They are full team members and compete on Goals, Kudos, SI and PTO, but they never move a Time tracked, Time planned or team RCR number, even when some of them do plan, track or bill in a given month. Their hours and RCR still appear on their own row, muted, for information. Scoring follows the same rule: an exempt rocketeer carries no tracked, planned or RCR weight.
👥 Managers vs individual contributors — inside the tracking mandate, the two cohorts are reported and judged separately. A rocketeer counts as a manager when their Job Catalog track is M or at least one roster member reports to them — so a newly-titled manager with no reports yet and an I-track lead who does manage people are both on the manager bar, never the IC one. A manager's week is coaching, escalation, hiring and 1:1s, work that never reaches Tempo, so measuring them on the IC yardstick reads as blame for doing the job. Both cohorts are still held to planning and tracking, at different bars: ICs 85% coverage · 36h target · 28h floor. Managers 70% coverage · 20h target · 10h floor for tracking, and 70% coverage for planning. The Executive Brief states each cohort's number on its own line and names the bar it was measured against; the IC line is the one that carries a support action, because that is where a gap costs delivery capacity. Planning is the exception where a manager gap is itself a concern — team capacity is unforecastable without it. Crew members are never classified as managers.

Metrics Explained
RCR — Revenue Contribution Rate
Team revenue divided by total cash compensation for the last closed month. A team-level indicator, not a measure of any individual. We aim to stay above 1× — read it as a directional check, not a hard target.
Updated monthly · reflects the last closed month shown at the top of the card.
SI — Small Improvements Rating
Quarterly manager rating on a 1–5 scale from the Small Improvements platform. The dashboard shows the last closed quarter (—) as a rounded integer — static until the next quarter closes.
Scale: 0 Not rated (excluded) · 1 Needs Help · 2 Below Expectation · 3 Meeting · 4 Great · 5 Outstanding
Goals connected
Weekly compliance check against current-quarter goals. A rocketeer is compliant when all three rules pass (see Goals Evaluation below). Each week is evaluated independently — goals drive planning, planning drives execution.
Available for weeks W-2 → W0 (current). Future weeks show —.
Time planned
Hours planned in Tempo for each week, shown across all 5 weeks including upcoming ones. Planning is a decision, not a default. Same population as Time tracked: ⏱ Exempt families and anyone on leave are excluded. Per-person planned hours are capped at the weekly capacity remaining after PTO — Tempo repeats a plan across every project a rocketeer is staffed on, which would otherwise inflate the total.
Available across all 5 weeks (past, current, and W+1, W+2).
Time tracked
Hours logged in Tempo, summed per ISO week (Mon–Sun) and matched to the roster. Covers the rocketeers held to the tracking mandate — ramp-up and active alike; ⏱ Exempt families and anyone on leave are excluded from both the hours and the headcount.
Available for weeks W-2 → W0 (current).
Kudos given
Count of Kudos received each week — a signal of how much the team sees and appreciates each other.
Available for weeks W-2 → W0 (current).
PTO — Planned Time Off
Each employee is either Planned (balance under control, or upcoming leave booked) or Needs a plan (high balance, no upcoming leave). Team score = % in Planned status, shown across all 5 weeks — a team that plans its recovery sustains its performance.
Available all 5 weeks including W+1 and W+2.
Goals Evaluation — current quarter

Each week, every rocketeer is evaluated against their current-quarter goals using that week's Mon–Sun window. To be marked Compliant, all three rules below must pass. Quarter target: 10 goals, 8 completed by quarter end.

1
Goal Count — at least 8 goals
At least 8 distinct goals in the quarter's goal set. During the first 4 weeks of a new quarter this rule is relaxed while goals are being written — the 8-goal minimum applies from quarter week 5.
2
In Progress — majority of goals actively in progress
More than 50% of goals must be In Progress. Planned or Completed goals do not count toward this rule — it checks active execution, not just goal setup.
3
Comment Engagement — at least 6 goals with cumulative self-comments
At least 6 goals must have the required number of cumulative self-comments by that week's end. Manager comments do not count. Completed goals qualify with a single self-comment, and a full-week PTO week exempts this rule.
Required comments per goal = max(1, quarterWeek − 4), where quarter week 1 = —: 1 comment through week 5, then one more each week (week 6 = 2, week 7 = 3, …). The ramp rewards steady engagement over a last-minute push.
Quarter override: a rocketeer whose goals are all Completed (8+ total), or who has 8+ Completed goals with 5 of them fully documented (8+ self-comments each), is marked compliant for every week of the quarter. Once a quarter has closed, anyone whose goals are all closed with 8+ Completed is likewise marked compliant for that whole quarter — Canceled or Not Completed leftovers don't change the signal. Mid-quarter, the weekly rules still require full compliance.
During the first 3 weeks of a new quarter, the previous quarter's final standing is carried alongside while new-quarter goals spin up. Expand any employee row to see exactly which rule failed and by how much.
Team Radar — Axis Definitions

The Team Radar (snowflake chart) plots seven axes on a 0–100 scale for any selected group and reference week. Each axis normalises its raw value differently. The "Evaluate Week" selector controls which week is used for single-week axes (Goals, Tracked compliance, Planned compliance, Kudos, PTO Health); period averages (Tracked avg, Planned avg) use the full 5-week window. In Month or Quarter mode (the Week | Month | Quarter toggle), single-week axes evaluate at the period's last closed week for standings (Goals, PTO Health) while flow axes (Tracked, Planned, Kudos) average across the period's closed weeks; the reference polygon compares against the prior month or quarter.

Week Range

All weekly data uses ISO 8601 week numbering — weeks run Monday to Sunday. Week labels follow the format YYYY.WN, for example 2026.W20 covers Mon 11 May – Sun 17 May 2026. The 5-week window is always centered on the current ISO week: two past weeks, the current week, and two future weeks. Months and fiscal quarters group weeks by the ISO 8601 majority rule — a week belongs to the month (or quarter) containing its Thursday, so no week is ever split. Fiscal quarters follow the FY calendar (FY starts July 1). Quarterly RCR is the mean of monthly values and is marked approximate.
